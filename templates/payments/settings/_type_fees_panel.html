Fees by institution category

Polytechnics, monotechnics, colleges of health, colleges of nursing and the other categories each have their own fees. Type the amounts in naira and press Save. New bills use them at once; bills already issued never change.

1 · Fill inEnter each fee. Leave 0 where a fee does not apply — that line is left off the bill.
2 · Switch on“In use” makes the category bill from this table. While off, the older ownership formula still applies.
3 · ConfirmTick “Confirmed by Finance” once the figures are approved. Unconfirmed rows show a warning to staff.
