Director of Finance review

Every payment claim and verified provider callback goes directly to the Finance Director.

Provider callbacks supply evidence only. They never confirm a payment automatically. The Director of Finance reviews the amount, supporting evidence, and any provider mismatch, then confirms or rejects the payment.

No preliminary officer approval or amount-based approval chain is used.
