Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel a QA request (accreditation, resource inspection and the others) at any time before NBTE's final decision, and your onboarding application before your institution is approved.

How much is refunded

The share of what you paid that comes back depends on how far NBTE's work has gone when you cancel.

{% include "payments/refunds/_policy_table.html" %}

How to cancel

  1. Open the request (or your application) and choose Cancel. Only the Chief Executive can.
  2. Check the refund shown, say why you are cancelling and tick the declaration.
  3. Give the bank account for the refund — then or later on Refunds.
  4. NBTE Finance checks it, it is approved, and the money is paid. You are notified at each step.
