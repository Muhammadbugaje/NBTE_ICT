On this page

Home shows what needs your action now, and what happened recently. Start with the card at the top: it always names your next step.

Guide: your dashboard

Your application

Follow the steps in order; each one unlocks the next. If a department returns your application, its reason is shown so you can correct and resubmit.

Read the Onboarding guide

Documents

Upload each required document as a PDF or image. A returned document shows NBTE's reason; upload a corrected version in its place.

Read the Onboarding guide

Payments

Every fee is a bill with its own reference. Pay online, or by bank transfer and upload your proof. A payment counts once NBTE Finance confirms it.

Read the Payments guide Your refunds · Cancellations and refunds

QA requests and SSQs

Choose the type of QA and the programmes. After the fee is confirmed, one self-study questionnaire opens per programme; you can send sections to departments.

Read the QA requests guide

Programmes

Each programme's status, approved streams and expiry date. They update automatically when NBTE issues a decision.

Read the QA requests guide

Queries

NBTE asks here when something is missing. Answer on the portal so your reply stays on the record and the review can continue.

Guide: how NBTE contacts you

Messages from NBTE

Notifications, memos and letters from NBTE. Open one to read it in full; memos that ask for it need an acknowledgement.

Guide: how NBTE contacts you

Profile and team

Keep your institution's details and users up to date. After your first application is submitted, changes go to NBTE as a change request.

Guide: your institution's record

Timeline

A permanent record of everything that happened on your institution's record, newest first. Use the filters to find an event.

Guide: the timeline

Account settings

Your name, password, notification settings and the devices you are signed in on. Only NBTE can change your e-mail address or role.

Guide: account settings

Inspections

NBTE's scheduled and recorded visits to your institution. You are told when a visit is scheduled, when it takes place and when the decision is issued.
