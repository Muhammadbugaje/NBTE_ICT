{# ============================== FAQ ============================== #}

Questions

{% site_text "portal.home.title.faq" %}

We are already registered with NBTE. Do we register again?

No. NBTE sets up existing institutions and e-mails a link to set your password. If you cannot find it, use Forgot password with your institution's registered e-mail address.

Who should hold the administrator account?

Usually the Registrar or the head of the QA unit. The administrator keeps the institution's record up to date, starts requests, pays fees and adds accounts for academic departments. Do not share the login; ask NBTE to change it if the person changes.

How do we pay, and when does a payment count?

Pay online with Remita or Paystack, or at the bank quoting the bill's payment reference and uploading the receipt as proof. A payment counts once NBTE Finance confirms it against the bank record; the next step then opens on its own.

Can our academic departments fill in parts of the SSQ?

Yes. Send the sections to the department from the SSQ page. The department completes them and sends the SSQ back to you to check and submit.

What happens after our SSQs are accepted?

NBTE opens an internal case: it assigns the team, costs and funds the visit, inspects, reports and decides. You do not need to act during these stages; you are told when the visit is approved, when it takes place and when the decision is made.

Does the portal work on a phone?

Yes, on any phone, tablet or computer. On a phone the main sections sit in a bar at the bottom of the screen.
