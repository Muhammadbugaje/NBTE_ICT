Getting started

Every institution has one institution administrator, usually the Registrar or the head of the QA unit. The administrator signs in, keeps the institution's record up to date, and can add department accounts for heads of academic departments, who complete their parts of each self-study questionnaire.

{% if registration_open %}

If your institution is new to NBTE, register it here. You will choose a password at the end and can start your application straight away. If NBTE has already registered your institution, do not register again: use the set-password link NBTE e-mailed to your official address.

{% else %}

NBTE registers new institutions. {{ registration_closed_message }} You will then receive an e-mail with a link to set your password.

{% endif %}

Sign in at any time on a phone, tablet or computer. Every request, payment, query and visit appears on your institution's timeline, and NBTE e-mails you when something needs your action.

Onboarding a new institution

for proposed institutions

Onboarding turns a proposed institution into an approved one. You submit your application with the required documents (for example your CAC certificate, enabling law or bank guarantee, depending on your ownership). Four NBTE departments clear it in parallel: Legal, Finance, Academic Planning and Physical Planning. If one of them returns it, you will see the reason and can correct and resubmit.

Once all four have cleared it, you pay the institutional fee. When NBTE Finance confirms the payment, you choose the programmes you plan to run. The NTIRD department clears each programme, you pay the programme fee and complete a self-study questionnaire, and NBTE schedules a resource inspection.

Requesting quality assurance

for approved institutions

Open QA requests and choose New request. Pick the type of QA and the programmes it covers; the portal only offers programmes that are eligible for that type, and tells you why any are not. Programmes you have never run before go to programme clearance first.

Submitting the request issues a bill for the QA fee. When Finance confirms your payment, one self-study questionnaire (SSQ) opens for each programme. You can fill it in yourself or send sections to the academic department that owns them. Evidence you upload once can be reused across SSQs.

NBTE reviews each SSQ. If something is missing, it raises a query: answer it on the portal and the review continues. A returned SSQ starts a new version with your earlier answers carried forward, so nothing is lost.

Until the fee is confirmed you can add, drop or replace programmes, or cancel the request.

What happens inside NBTE

after your SSQs are accepted

Once your questionnaires are accepted, NBTE plans the visit. You do not need to do anything while this runs. You will be told at the moments that matter to you: when the visit is scheduled, when it takes place, and when the decision is issued.

After the visit NBTE reviews the team's report and issues the decision. Your programme's record (status, approved streams and expiry date) updates automatically.

Paying fees

Every fee starts as a bill on your Payments page, with its own reference. Pay online with Remita or Paystack, or by bank transfer to the NBTE account printed on the bill. For a bank transfer, use Upload proof of payment on the bill to add your teller or transfer receipt.

A payment only counts once NBTE Finance confirms it against the bank record. An online provider saying “paid” is not enough on its own. When Finance confirms, your receipt is issued and the next step opens automatically. Each receipt carries a QR code anyone can scan to check it is genuine.

Joint QA with professional bodies

Some programmes are also regulated by a professional council, for example ND Pharmacy Technician by the Pharmacy Council of Nigeria. When your request includes such a programme, it becomes a joint QA exercise: the portal marks it on your request, and NBTE tells the council when your case opens so its assessor can join the visit team.

You do not need to apply to the council separately through the portal. Any fee or paperwork the council requires is arranged directly with them.

Questions

Who should hold the institution administrator account?

Someone senior who will be there for the whole process, usually the Registrar or the head of the QA unit. If they leave, ask NBTE to update your institution's contact and send login details to their successor, using the contact details below.

Can academic departments fill in the SSQ?

Yes. The administrator creates a department account, then sends that department the SSQ sections it owns. When the department is done, the SSQ comes back to the administrator, who checks it and submits it to NBTE.

I paid but the portal still says the fee is due.

NBTE Finance confirms every payment against the bank record before it counts. If you paid by bank transfer, make sure you uploaded the proof on the bill's page. The status changes to confirmed as soon as Finance matches it.

What if NBTE returns my SSQ?

You will see NBTE's remark on the SSQ. Choose Revise to start a new version: your earlier answers and evidence are carried forward, so you only change what was asked.

How will I know the outcome?

When the Executive Secretary gives final approval, the decision appears on your timeline, NBTE e-mails your official address, and your programme's record shows its new status and expiry date.
