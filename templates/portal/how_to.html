Overview

Three things live in the Portal

Announcements, events, and reminders. Each has its own page, but they all share the same visibility system — you pick who should see something, and it appears only for them.

Announcements Notices with images and links. Auto-expire.
Events Meetings with time, place, RSVP, and .ics.
Reminders Nudges to yourself, a dept, or everyone.

{# ----- Step 1: Announcement ----- #}
Step 1 of 3

Post your first announcement

Announcements are for one-off information: notices, reminders, guides. You can add an image, labeled links, an expiry date, and pin the important ones to the top.

  1. 1
    Go to Announcements → New announcement.

    The button is at the top right of the list.

    Open the form
  2. 2
    Fill in title, summary, body.

    The summary is what shows on cards and the home dashboard.

  3. 3
    Add labeled links

    Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, or a document. Each row takes a kind, a label, and a URL.

  4. 4
    Pick visibility.

    Public, all staff, department, specific roles, or specific people. The form shows only the fields that matter.

  5. 5
    Set publish and expiry times.

    Leave expiry blank for announcements that should stay live.

Tip

Pin important announcements. They stay at the very top of every list for as long as they're live.

{# ----- Step 2: Event ----- #}
Step 2 of 3

Schedule your first event

Events are like announcements but they have a start and end time, an optional location, an optional virtual link, and RSVP tracking.

  1. 1
    Go to Events → New event. Open the form
  2. 2
    Set the time.

    Starts at, ends at (optional), all-day if it spans the whole day.

  3. 3
    Location and virtual link.

    Either, both, or neither. Virtual links appear as "Join online" on the detail page.

  4. 4
    RSVP (optional).

    When enabled, people can respond Going / Maybe / Not going.

  5. 5
    Save.

    Staff can add the event to their calendar with the Add to calendar button — it downloads an .ics file.

Event status is computed live

Events move from Upcoming → Ongoing → Past automatically. You never have to update anything.

{# ----- Step 3: Reminder ----- #}
Step 3 of 3

Set a reminder

Reminders fire an in-app notification at the time you pick. You can send one to yourself, to a department, to specific roles, or to specific people.

  1. 1
    Go to Reminders → New reminder. Open the form
  2. 2
    Write the title and body.

    The title becomes the notification heading; the body is the message.

  3. 3
    Choose who receives it.

    The scope dropdown drives which fields appear next to it. Personal hides everything else; Custom reveals role and people pickers.

  4. 4
    Set the remind-at time.

    The notification goes out at that moment, to everyone in scope.

⏰
Recurring?

Set up a daily or weekly reminder by re-saving it after it fires. Recurrence is on the roadmap.

Who can publish what

Not every account can create everything. Here's the breakdown — you'll see the buttons only if you have permission.

Role Can publish Can pin Public site
Director / Deputy Everything Yes Yes

Media team Announcements, events for own dept Yes No
Everyone else Own reminders No No
Need access?

Ask a Director or Deputy Director to grant you the media-team role.
