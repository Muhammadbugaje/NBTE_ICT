{# ============================ HELP + SAMPLE ============================ #}

Expected format

| Email            | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | ... |
|------------------|---|---|---|---|---|-----|
| ali@nbte.gov.ng  | 1 | 1 | 0 | 1 | L | ... |
| fatima@nbte.ng   | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 1 | ... |
  • 1 or P — Present
  • L — Late
  • 0 or A — Absent
  • E — Excused
  • H — Holiday
  • OD — On official duty (for example a QA visit)
  • Times are optional: 1 08:05-16:40 records the check-in and check-out; L 09:20- means no check-out. Exports use the same format, so an export can be uploaded again.
  • Present, Late, Excused and Holiday days are all credited towards attendance; only Absent days are not. Approved leave (LV) is filled in automatically from approved leave requests.
  • Blank cell — skip (no record created)
