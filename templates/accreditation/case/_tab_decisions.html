{% if cqac_recommendation_mode == "individual" %}

Every assigned panelist votes on each programme. The outcome is decided by the full panel; approval type, stream count, and commencement date are each decided by majority among members who voted “Approve.” If any detail has no majority, it is returned for review. The stage cannot advance until all active assigned panel members have voted.

{% else %}

The CQAC acts as one panel. Any assigned panel member may record the shared recommendation for each programme; the Chairman reviews it at the next stage.
