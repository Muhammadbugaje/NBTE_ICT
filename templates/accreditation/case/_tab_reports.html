12

Post-visit reports

Stage 12 has two separate jobs. Each attendee writes their own report for the programme they covered. Only the Team Lead reviews those reports and writes the one compiled programme report that goes for endorsement. Attendees cannot submit the compiled report; the Team Lead cannot submit on an attendee's behalf.
