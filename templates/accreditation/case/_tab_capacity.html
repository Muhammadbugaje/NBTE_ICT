Capacity and resources

Capacity work starts alongside the post-visit report, as soon as the visit is recorded. The assigned desk officer checks and records the verified resources for every programme; the owning department Director then confirms each assessment at Stage 13 before the case can proceed to CQAC.

1Assess · assigned desk officer

Complete this work while post-visit reports are being prepared. Check the submitted SSQ against visit evidence; verify and save all five resource sections.

2Confirm · owning department Director

Review the saved figures and results for each programme, then confirm the current assessment set.

3Proceed · CQAC

Stage 13 cannot move forward until every programme is assessed and Director-confirmed. Any shortfall remains visible to CQAC, the Chairman and ES.
