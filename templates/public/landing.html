What it is

{% site_text "home.title.about" %}

Instead of scattered forms, phone calls and paper, everything the {{ site_info.department_name }} needs to run day-to-day operations lives in one place.

Helpdesk and tickets

Report an IT issue in seconds. Every ticket is assigned, tracked against clear service targets and escalated automatically if the clock runs out.

Every ticket has an owner

Asset custody

Every device, its current custodian and the full issue and return history with condition tracking, so nothing goes missing.

Labels with QR codes

Staff directory

A living record of everyone: roles, reporting lines and the work assigned to them.

Governed approvals

Leave, proposals and requests move through a multi-step sign-off with a complete audit trail.

Live analytics

Directors and heads of office see workload, service targets and trends as they change.

Internal portal

Announcements, events, documents, the gallery and awards: the everyday content that keeps a directorate together.

How it works

{% site_text "home.title.how" %}

  1. Report or request

    Raise a ticket, request leave or log an asset movement from any device.

  2. Routed automatically

    The right office sees it at once, with priority and service target set by the rules you configured.

  3. Worked and tracked

    The assigned officer resolves it. Escalations fire automatically if the target is missed.

  4. Recorded for good

    Every action, comment and decision is kept in an audit trail you can hand to anyone.
