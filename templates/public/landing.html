What it is

One workspace for the whole directorate.

Instead of scattered forms, phone calls, and paper — everything the ICT Directorate needs to run day-to-day operations lives in one place.

Helpdesk & Tickets

Report an IT issue in seconds. Tracked, assigned, and escalated against clear SLAs — every ticket has an owner.

Asset custody

Every device, its current custodian, and the full issue/return history with condition tracking — nothing goes missing.

Staff directory

A living record of everyone in the directorate — roles, reporting lines, current state, and their assigned work.

Governed approvals

Leave, proposals, and internal requests move through a configurable multi-step sign-off with a complete audit trail.

Live analytics

Directors and office heads see the numbers that matter — SLA compliance, workload, and trends — as they change.

Internal portal

Announcements, events, documents, gallery, awards — the everyday content that keeps a department together.

How it works

From report to resolution, in four clean steps.

1

Report or request

Raise a ticket, submit a leave request, or log an asset movement from anywhere.

2

Routed automatically

The right office sees it instantly, with priority and SLA already set by the rules you configured.

3

Worked & tracked

Assigned officers resolve it. Escalations fire automatically if the SLA clock runs out.

4

Recorded forever

Every action, comment, and decision is preserved in an auditable trail you can hand to anyone.
