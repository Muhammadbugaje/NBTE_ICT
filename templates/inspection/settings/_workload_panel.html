How a person's row is chosen

  1. Their designation (for example Chief Lecturer), or an external resource person's rank code.
  2. Their role (for example Director).
  3. Their kind: QA personnel, resource person or NBTE staff.
  4. Otherwise Everyone else.

The finder ranks people with the fewest assignments this year first, then the longest since they last served. Current loads are shown in the finder and in the QA personnel and resource-person lists.
