## 2. Memo routing fixed

The full explanation, rules table, before/after comparison and commands are at the top of **MEMO_ROUTING.md**. In short:
- a director → own department or another director: **delivered directly**;
- a director → another directorate's staff: **that director approves first**;
- staff: line head → deputy → own director, then the receiving director when the memo leaves the department;
- each step is skipped when nobody holds it, when the sender is at that level or above, or when the approver is the recipient.
